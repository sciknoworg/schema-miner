FEEDBACK:
Highly granular schema with 9 top-level properties, all required. The product_composition section with detailed compound-concentration pairs for both liquid and gas fractions is the most detailed among all schemas.

Property Merging: "plastic_feedstock_types" and "feedstock_composition" should be merged into a single "feedstock" object. Having them as separate top-level arrays is redundant — the composition array already contains polymer names. Recommendation: merge into a "feedstock" object with "types" (array) and "composition" (array of {polymer, proportion} objects).

Property Grouping: The detailed compound-concentration structure in product_composition matches how GC-MS results are reported in papers — this is excellent design.

Missing Properties: (1) No feedstock preprocessing. (2) No catalyst characterization (BET, NH3-TPD, XRD, SEM). (3) No metadata (study ID, DOI). (4) No product quality properties (HHV, viscosity). (5) No pressure property. (6) No carrier gas flow rate. (7) The residence_time maximum of 60 minutes is too restrictive — batch pyrolysis experiments commonly run 90 min (Sivagami et al. 2021, Paper 8) and up to 120 min. Should be at least 120 min. (8) No mass balance closure property.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Good with helpful constraint notes ("Sum of proportions must equal 1.0", "Sum must be ≤100%"). The analytical_methods enum includes GC-FID, DSC, XRD — broader than most schemas.
