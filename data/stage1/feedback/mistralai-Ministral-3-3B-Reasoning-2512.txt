FEEDBACK:
The weakest schema structurally with only 3 top-level properties (process_name, input_parameters, output_parameters). All parameters are flattened inside input/output blocks without subgrouping.

Property Merging: The entire input_parameters block should be decomposed into separate objects: feedstock, catalyst, reactor, process_conditions. Similarly, output_parameters should separate yields, product_composition, and analytical_methods. This restructuring is essential for usability.

Property Grouping: All 10 input parameters are at the same nesting level with no subgrouping. This makes the schema harder to navigate and less useful for structured data extraction from papers.

Missing Properties: (1) No metadata. (2) No feedstock preprocessing. (3) No catalyst characterization. (4) No product quality (HHV, viscosity). (5) No pressure. (6) No carrier gas flow rate. (7) No mass balance closure. (8) JSON structural error: under gas_fraction items, "unit" is defined as a string value ("mole fraction") rather than a proper schema property with type.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Individual descriptions are adequate. The feedstock_composition structure with "type", "quantity", and "unit" fields is well-designed with clear indication of measurement units.
