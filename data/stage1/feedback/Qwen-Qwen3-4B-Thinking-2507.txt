FEEDBACK:
Compact but effective schema with 5 top-level properties, all required. Good balance between simplicity and completeness for a thinking model.

Property Merging: The "process_parameters" groups temperature, heating rate, residence time, atmosphere, reactor configuration, and operational scale together. Consider separating reactor_configuration and operational_scale into a "reactor" or "experimental_setup" object, as they describe the physical setup rather than operating conditions.

Missing Properties: (1) No metadata. (2) No feedstock preprocessing. (3) No catalyst characterization (BET, NH3-TPD, XRD, SEM). (4) No product quality (HHV 37-45 MJ/kg, viscosity). (5) No pressure. (6) No carrier gas flow rate. (7) No mass balance closure. (8) Product composition is a flat array of compound names without concentrations — less informative than compound-concentration pairs.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Clear with helpful examples. The constraint about yield percentages summing to 100% is useful. The schema description referencing SBERT and Neo4j comes from the process.txt rather than being generic schema design.
