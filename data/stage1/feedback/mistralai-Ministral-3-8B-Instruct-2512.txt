"1. There is some redundancy between moc_type.formula and the more granular breakdown into metal_ions, ligands, and counter_ions. While both are useful, the schema could define a clearer relationship to avoid inconsistency. 

process_parameters.steps.conditions contains repeated structures (temperature, pressure, time) that could be abstracted into a reusable “conditions” definition. 

Minor overlap also exists between other_properties.adsorption and host_guest_properties in terms of functional performance, which could be partially unified.

2. The schema is logically structured but could benefit from clearer higher-level grouping. metadata and moc_type together form a “cage_identity” block. reactants and process_parameters naturally group into a “synthesis_protocol” unit. Meanwhile, characterization, host_guest_properties, and other_properties could be grouped under a “post_synthesis_characterization” or “evaluation” block to clearly separate synthesis from analysis and application.

3.Missing Properties: 
stoichiometric ratios in the reaction mixture
reaction scale 
Purification methods 

topology (e.g., cage geometry, connectivity), types of metals and coordination environment are not explicitly defined

4. The descriptions are generally clear, detailed, and more standardized than previous schemas, especially with consistent use of units and nested condition objects. However, some flexibility may introduce ambiguity (e.g., free-text results in characterization, loosely defined parameters). The balance between structured fields and free text could be improved for better interoperability.
The 3D spatial considerations of sterics, i.e. chair vs boat, is not required."
