FEEDBACK:
Well-structured schema with unique features like "performance_metrics" (conversion_efficiency, energy_efficiency, catalyst_lifetime) that no other model captures. The "feedstock_preparation" property under feedstock is also unique and important.

Property Merging: The reactor object contains "reaction_conditions" as a sub-object with temperature, heating_rate, residence_time, atmosphere, pressure. This deep nesting is logical but may make data extraction harder. Consider keeping conditions at a higher level.

Property Grouping: The analytical_methods section with separate feedstock/liquid/gas/solid analysis subsections is well-designed and reflects laboratory practice.

Missing Properties: (1) Feedstock composition should be required (currently only plastic_types is required). (2) No explicit property for carrier gas flow rate. (3) Operational scale should be required, not optional. (4) The catalyst_properties sub-object (surface_area, pore_volume, acidity) is excellent — should add characterization methods (BET, NH3-TPD, XRD, SEM) to document how these were measured.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Clear and well-written. The "solid_residue_analysis" under products is a good addition for char characterization.

ADDITIONAL COMMENTS:
The "performance_metrics" section is unique and valuable — conversion_efficiency, energy_efficiency, and catalyst_lifetime are increasingly reported in modern pyrolysis studies. However, few required properties (only feedstock, catalyst, reactor, products) means the schema is very permissive and may lead to incomplete data entries.
