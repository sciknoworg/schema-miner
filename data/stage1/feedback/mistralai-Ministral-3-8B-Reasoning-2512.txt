FEEDBACK:
Clean and well-organized schema with 5 top-level properties, all required. All essential aspects are covered with good structural organization.

Property Merging: Temperature, heating_rate, and residence_time are grouped under "reactor" rather than a separate "process_conditions" object. This is valid but less common in the literature where reactor hardware and operating conditions are typically discussed separately. Consider separating.

Missing Properties: (1) No metadata. (2) No feedstock preprocessing. (3) No catalyst characterization. (4) No product quality (HHV, viscosity). (5) No pressure. (6) No carrier gas flow rate. (7) No mass balance closure. (8) The analytical methods enum is too restrictive — only GC-MS, FTIR, TGA, NMR, HPLC, "other". Missing GC-FID, GCxGC, BET, NH3-TPD, XRD, SEM, bomb calorimetry.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Clear. IMPORTANT: The yield properties use 0-1 range (proportion) rather than 0-100 (percentage). This should be standardized to 0-100 percentages as this matches how values are reported in pyrolysis papers.
