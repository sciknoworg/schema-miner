FEEDBACK:
The schema captures the essential structure of catalytic pyrolysis with 8 well-organized top-level properties. However, several improvements are needed based on domain expertise in catalytic pyrolysis of mixed plastic waste:

Property Merging: The "conditions" and "reactor" objects should be grouped together since reactor configuration, temperature, heating rate, residence time, and atmosphere are all part of the experimental setup. Consider merging into a single "experimental_setup" or keeping reactor separate but moving atmosphere into reactor.

Property Grouping: The "outputs" section correctly groups yields and composition together. This is a good design.

Missing Properties: (1) Feedstock preparation/pretreatment method (shredding, washing, drying) is missing — this significantly affects results in real mixed waste streams. (2) Catalyst characterization properties are absent (BET surface area, acidity via NH3-TPD, pore structure via XRD, morphology via SEM). (3) Product quality metrics such as HHV (higher heating value, typically 37-45 MJ/kg), viscosity, density of liquid oil are not captured. (4) The "analysis" enum is too restrictive — only GC-MS, FTIR, TGA, and "Other". Important techniques like GC-FID, GCxGC-FID/MS, BET, NH3-TPD, XRD, SEM, bomb calorimetry, and ASTM fuel property tests (D2887, D4868, D4294) are missing. (5) No property for carrier gas flow rate. (6) No property for pressure. (7) Residence time is listed in seconds, but the literature predominantly reports it in minutes. (8) No mass balance closure property. (9) Feedstock "type" enum should include LDPE, HDPE, EPS, HIPS, PVC, and MLP (multilayer packaging) as separate entries.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Descriptions are clear but brief. The feedstock "type" enum using just "PE" is insufficient — LDPE and HDPE have significantly different degradation profiles and product distributions.
