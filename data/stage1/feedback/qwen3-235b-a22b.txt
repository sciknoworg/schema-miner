Method 1	

"1. There is redundancy between ligand_synthesis.steps.parameters and process_parameters.steps.parameters. These should be merged into a reusable definition such as reaction_conditions, referenced in both sections. Similarly, characterisation and other_properties.additional_characterization could be unified under a broader characterization structure with optional subtypes.

2. reactants would benefit from grouping into a higher-level unit such as components, with subgroups: metal_sources, ligands, and counter_ions. This better reflects chemical intuition. Additionally, host_guest and parts of other_properties could be grouped into a functional_properties unit describing post-synthetic behaviour.

3. Key omissions include:

yield (e.g., % or isolated mass)
scale (reaction scale, e.g., mmol)
crystallisation_method (diffusion, slow evaporation, etc.)
purification_method (filtration, recrystallisation)

4. Adequacy of Descriptions
Descriptions are generally clear and domain-aware, but sometimes overly verbose and inconsistent in specificity. Units are well constrained, but some fields (e.g., details) are too free-text, reducing standardisation. Greater use of controlled vocabularies would improve downstream usability."
