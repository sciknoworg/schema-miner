process name: Metal-organic cages synthesis
process description: Metal–organic cages (MOCs) are synthesized through coordination-driven self-assembly, where metal ions and multitopic organic ligands are mixed in solution under controlled conditions. The components spontaneously organize via reversible coordination bonds into discrete, well-defined cage-like structures with internal cavities. Reaction parameters such as solvent, temperature, and stoichiometry influence the final geometry, size, and yield of the cages
