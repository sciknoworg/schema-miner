FEEDBACK:
The schema shows significant improvement from Batch 1. Metadata, feedstock preprocessing (structured with method and parameters), feedstock contaminants (with element enum and concentration_ppm), catalyst characterization methods, product quality, and a comprehensive analytical methods enum are all now present.

Property Merging: No merging needed.

Property Grouping: Operating conditions remain inside reactor.operational_conditions rather than in a separate process_conditions object — separating hardware from operating parameters was requested in Batch 1 and has not been done.

Missing Properties — PREVIOUSLY REQUESTED AND STILL NOT INCORPORATED: (1) Separating operating conditions (temperature, heating_rate, residence_time, atmosphere) into a standalone process_conditions object was requested in Batch 1 — these are still inside reactor.operational_conditions. (2) Feedstock composition has a structural JSON Schema error — "percentage" is defined as a number type with additionalProperties inside it, which is invalid. This was flagged in Batch 1 and has not been corrected. (3) Product composition stores compound names only as plain strings without concentrations — adding compound-concentration pairs was requested in Batch 1 and has not been applied. (4) A simple mass_balance_closure percentage field was requested in Batch 1 — the schema has a mass_balance object (input_mass, output_mass, losses) but no simple percentage closure field, which is how it is universally reported in the literature.

Missing Properties — NEW: (5) No char composition (carbon_content_pct, ash_content_pct) in the products section — consistently reported in batch 2 papers.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Clear. The contaminant tracking with element enum (Cd, Pb, Cr, Ni, Zn) and concentration_ppm is the most precise design among all 12 schemas and must be preserved.
