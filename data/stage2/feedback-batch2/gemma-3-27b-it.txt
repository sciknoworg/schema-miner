FEEDBACK:
The schema has incorporated most Batch 1 feedback well. Metadata (study_id, author, DOI, publication_year), feedstock contaminants, structured product composition with compound-concentration arrays, product quality, and expanded analytical methods with ASTM standards are all now present.

Property Merging: No merging needed. The structure is logical and well-maintained.

Property Grouping: Product composition is correctly inside outputs alongside yields and quality. Analysis array at top level is appropriate.

Missing Properties — PREVIOUSLY REQUESTED AND STILL NOT INCORPORATED: (1) The analysis array is still not listed under "required" — making it required was explicitly requested in Batch 1 feedback. A valid instance can still be submitted with no analytical methods. (2) Temperature minimum is still 0 instead of 300°C — this was flagged in Batch 1 as too permissive for catalytic pyrolysis. (3) Mass balance closure maximum is still 100 — this was flagged in Batch 1 as too strict since experimental error typically gives closure of 95-105%. Maximum should be at least 105 or 110. (4) Carrier gas flow rate was requested in Batch 1 — it has been added to experimental_setup but its description says "mL/min" without making explicit that L/min is also common. This should clarify units.

Missing Properties — NEW:  No char composition (carbon_content_pct, ash_content_pct) in outputs — consistently reported in the batch 2 papers.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Clear. The feedstock type enum remains the most domain-complete among all 12 schemas (LDPE, HDPE, EPS, HIPS, PVC, MLP) and must be preserved in Stage 3.
