FEEDBACK:
The schema remains the strongest overall and has incorporated the key Batch 1 feedback. Carrier gas flow rate is correctly included inside process_conditions.carrier_gas with both type and flow_rate. The experimental_setup has been simplified with number_of_replicates. All other unique features (catalyst deactivation, char composition, liquid_quality) are retained.

Property Merging: No merging needed. Structure remains excellent.

Property Grouping: The separation of feedstock, catalyst, process_conditions, experimental_setup, yields, product_composition, characterization, and metadata continues to be the best design among all 12 schemas.

Missing Properties — PREVIOUSLY REQUESTED AND STILL NOT INCORPORATED: (1) Temperature minimum was suggested to be raised from 250°C to 300°C in Batch 1 feedback — it is still 250°C. Catalytic pyrolysis does not practically occur below 300°C and the lower bound should reflect this. (2) Temperature maximum was suggested to be lowered from 800°C to 700°C in Batch 1 — it is now 900°C, which has increased rather than decreased. No papers in the corpus report catalytic pyrolysis of mixed plastic waste above 700°C. (3) The feedstock.composition patternProperties regex (^(PE|PP|PS|PET|PVC|[A-Z]{2,4})$) was flagged in Batch 1 as incorrectly rejecting LDPE, HDPE, EPS, HIPS, and MLP — this has not been corrected and will still reject these valid polymer types.

Missing Properties — NEW: (4) The gas product compound enum is too restrictive (H2, CH4, C2H4, C2H6, C3H6, C3H8, C4H10 only) — CO and CO2 appear in several batch 2 papers and should be added.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Scientifically the most precise of all 12 schemas. The catalyst deactivation properties (coke_formation_rate, deactivation_mechanism, reusability_cycles) and char composition (carbon_content, ash_content, catalyst_content) are uniquely valuable.
