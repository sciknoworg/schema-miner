FEEDBACK:
The schema continues to be one of the top two overall. Feedstock preprocessing is now present inside feedstock, moistureContent is correctly optional, char composition (carbon_content_pct, ash_content_pct, chlorine_ppm) has been added under productComposition, and the carrierGasFlowRate ambiguity has been resolved with a unified field and clear description.

Property Merging: No merging needed. The camelCase naming convention is consistently maintained.

Property Grouping: The separation of feedstock, catalyst, reactor, reactionConditions, productYields, productComposition, productQuality, analyticalMethods, and metadata is excellent for structured data extraction.

Missing Properties - PREVIOUSLY REQUESTED AND STILL NOT INCORPORATED: (1) Feedstock source was made required in Batch 1 — many papers in the Stage 3 corpus of 50-70 papers will not report feedstock source explicitly. This was flagged in Batch 1 feedback and making source optional was recommended. It is still required. (2) The analyticalMethods.result field was flagged in Batch 1 as too strict for data extraction from papers — requiring a result string for each method entry is impractical during schema population from literature. Making result optional was recommended in Batch 1 and has not been done.

Missing Properties - NEW: (3) The reactionConditions.atmosphere enum does not include a generic "inert" option, which would allow entries that specify inert atmosphere without identifying the specific gas. (4) The gas product heatingValue field per compound is innovative but rarely reported per individual component - making it optional (which it already is) is correct, but its description could clarify that it refers to the mixture rather than individual components.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Excellent. The char sub-object in productComposition and the gas quality metrics (CO2 content, sulfur content) in productQuality are uniquely valuable.
