FEEDBACK:
The schema remains stable and well-structured. Feedstock contaminants, catalyst characterization methods, structured product composition, product quality, and mass balance closure are all correctly maintained from Batch 1.

Property Merging: No merging needed. The structure is clean and consistent.

Property Grouping: Appropriate throughout. The analysis section remains as an object with a methods array inside rather than a flat top-level array, which is slightly inconsistent with other schemas.

Missing Properties - PREVIOUSLY REQUESTED AND STILL NOT INCORPORATED: (1) Temperature maximum of 600°C was flagged in Batch 1 as too low - the batch 2 papers include experiments at 650°C. Still not raised to 700°C. (2) Char composition (carbon_content_pct, ash_content_pct) was requested in Batch 1 and is still absent from the products section — it is consistently reported in both batch 1 and batch 2 papers. (3) Feedstock particle size and moisture content were requested in Batch 1 and remain absent -- both are reported in several batch 2 papers. (4) The DOI format restriction ("format": "uri") was flagged in Batch 1 as rejecting plain DOI strings — this has not been corrected and will still reject entries like "10.1016/j.jhazmat.2024.132456" without the full URL prefix.

Missing Properties - NEW: (5) No char sub-object in product composition (liquid and gas arrays are present but char has no structured entry alongside them).

Adequacy of Descriptions: Clear and concise. The expanded analytical methods enum and catalyst characterization methods array are well-designed and correctly maintained.
