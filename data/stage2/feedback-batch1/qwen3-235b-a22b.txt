This remains the strongest schema overall. Stage 1 feedback has been comprehensively incorporated: feedstock preprocessing, feedstock contaminants (chlorine_content_ppm, heavy_metals_ppm), product quality (HHV, chlorine, density, viscosity) under liquid_quality, char composition (carbon_content, ash_content, catalyst_content), and catalyst deactivation properties (reusability_cycles, deactivation_mechanism, coke_formation_rate) are all now present. The three-level characterization (feedstock_analysis, product_analysis, catalyst_analysis) remains the best analytical methods design among all 12 schemas.

Property Merging: No merging needed. The current grouping is logical and well-structured.

Property Grouping: The separation of feedstock, catalyst, process_conditions, experimental_setup, yields, product_composition, characterization, and metadata is ideal and reflects how data is actually organized and reported in catalytic pyrolysis papers.

Missing Properties: (1) Carrier gas flow rate is still absent — it should be added inside process_conditions.carrier_gas as a flow_rate field. (2) The experimental_setup.validation_method and replication_count are innovative but rarely reported in pyrolysis papers — consider making these fully optional and adding a simpler "number_of_replicates" integer.

Adequacy of Descriptions: The most scientifically precise descriptions of all 12 schemas. The catalyst subtype field (e.g., HZSM-5, Y-zeolite, Al-MCM-41) is an important addition for specificity. Temperature maximum should be raised from 800°C to match — or the minimum could be lowered slightly to 250°C to capture some low-temperature catalytic studies.
