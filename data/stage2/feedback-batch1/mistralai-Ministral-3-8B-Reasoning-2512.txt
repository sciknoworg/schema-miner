FEEDBACK:
The schema shows meaningful improvement from Stage 1. The most important fix — correcting yield scale from 0-1 fraction to 0-100 percentage — has been applied correctly. The mass_balance object now tracks input_mass, output_mass, and recovery_efficiency, which is a unique and valuable addition. The analytical methods enum has been expanded.

Property Merging: No merging needed. The top-level structure across feedstock, catalyst, reactor, products, and analytical_characterisation is clean.

Property Grouping: Operating conditions (temperature, heating_rate, residence_time, atmosphere) remain nested inside reactor.operational_conditions rather than in a separate process_conditions object — this mixes hardware description with thermal parameters and should be separated. The feedstock composition property has a structural issue: "percentage" is defined as a single number with additionalProperties inside it, which is incorrect JSON Schema syntax. It should be a plain object with additionalProperties at the top level.

Missing Properties: (1) Metadata (study_id, author, DOI) is still absent. (2) No feedstock preprocessing. (3) No feedstock contaminant tracking (chlorine_ppm, heavy metals). (4) No carrier gas flow rate. (5) Catalyst characterization methods (BET, NH3-TPD, XRD, SEM) are still absent. (6) Product quality (HHV, viscosity, density) is not captured.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Clear. Temperature maximum should be raised from 600°C to 700°C to reflect the full range used in the literature.
