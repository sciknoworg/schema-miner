FEEDBACK:
The schema remains one of the strongest, with unique features like performance_metrics (conversion_efficiency, energy_efficiency, catalyst_lifetime), solid_residue_analysis under products, and feedstock contaminant tracking (chlorine_ppm, heavy metals). Stage 1 feedback was well incorporated: catalyst characterization methods (BET, NH3-TPD, XRD, SEM, FT-IR, TGA, ICP-OES) are now present, contaminants are tracked in both feedstock and solid residue, and the analytical methods are split by sample type (feedstock, liquid, gas, solid) which is excellent practice reflecting real laboratory workflow.

Property Merging: No merging needed. The current structure across feedstock, catalyst, reactor, products, analytical_methods, and performance_metrics is logical and comprehensive.

Property Grouping: The deep nesting of temperature, heating_rate, residence_time, and atmosphere inside reactor is valid but makes data extraction harder. Consider separating these into a standalone process_conditions object at the same level as reactor.

Missing Properties: (1) No carrier gas flow rate — should be added to the reactor object. (2) Feedstock composition is not required — only plastic_types is required, but composition percentages are essential for reproducibility and should be made required. (3) Operational scale (laboratory/pilot/industrial) should be required, not optional.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Clear and well-written throughout. The solid_residue_analysis with carbon_content, ash_content, and contaminants is excellent and unique among the 12 schemas.
