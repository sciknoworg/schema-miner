FEEDBACK:
The schema shows good improvement from Stage 1. The feedstock structure has been correctly merged into a single object with types, composition, and preparation arrays. The residence_time maximum has been raised to 120 minutes. Catalyst characterization (BET_surface_area, NH3_TPD, XRD, SEM) now has a dedicated object. Product composition includes structured compound-concentration pairs for both liquid and gas fractions. Product quality (HHV, viscosity) and mass balance closure are present.

Property Merging: The catalyst_characterization is a separate top-level object rather than nested inside the catalyst object — these should be merged. Having catalyst properties at the top level alongside feedstock, reactor, and process_parameters creates an inconsistent hierarchy.

Property Grouping: The grouping across feedstock, catalyst, reactor, process_parameters, product_yields, product_composition, product_quality, mass_balance_closure, analytical_methods, metadata, and operational_scale is mostly logical. Moving catalyst_characterization inside catalyst would improve consistency.

Missing Properties: (1) Metadata is minimal (study_id and DOI only) — missing author and publication year. (2) No feedstock contaminant tracking (chlorine_ppm, heavy metals) — critical for PVC-containing waste. (3) No feedstock particle size or moisture content. (4) The analytical_methods enum is still restrictive — missing GCxGC-FID/MS, BET, NH3-TPD, SEM, bomb calorimetry, ASTM fuel tests. (5) Product quality is missing density and chlorine content of the liquid product.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Clear and include helpful constraint notes ("Sum of proportions must equal 1.0", "Sum must be ≤100%"). Temperature maximum should be raised from 600°C to 700°C.
