FEEDBACK:
This schema shows minimal structural improvement from Stage 1 and remains the weakest of the 12. The flat input_parameters/output_parameters structure persists, with catalyst, reactor, and process_conditions all incorrectly nested inside feedstock as siblings of feedstock properties rather than being independent top-level objects. The JSON schema itself contains structural errors — properties such as "preparation", "reactor", and "process_conditions" are defined as siblings of the "properties" keyword rather than inside it, meaning they would be ignored by a JSON Schema validator.

Property Merging: The entire input_parameters block should be decomposed: catalyst, reactor, and process_conditions should each be promoted to top-level objects alongside feedstock. Similarly, output_parameters should be split into separate yields, product_composition, product_quality, and analytical_methods objects.

Property Grouping: All 10 input parameters are at the same nesting level with no meaningful subgrouping. This makes the schema difficult to navigate and prevents structured data extraction from scientific papers.

Missing Properties: (1) Metadata (study_id, author, DOI) is absent. (2) No feedstock preprocessing. (3) No catalyst characterization methods. (4) No product quality (HHV, viscosity). (5) No feedstock contaminant tracking. (6) Mass balance closure is misplaced inside feedstock.composition rather than at the top level or products level.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Individual property descriptions are adequate where present. The feedstock_composition structure with "type", "quantity", and "unit" fields is well-designed with clear measurement units.
