FEEDBACK:
This remains one of the most comprehensive schemas, with unique and valuable properties incorporated following Stage 1: feedstock contaminants, feedstock preprocessing, reactor material and heat_transfer, and performance_metrics (carbon_conversion_efficiency, energy_efficiency, selectivity). The feedstock particle_size and moisture_content properties are excellent additions not seen in most other schemas.

Property Merging: No merging needed. The 8-property structure across feedstock, catalyst, reactor, operating_conditions, outputs, product_composition, analysis, and performance_metrics is well-organized.

Property Grouping: The grouping is appropriate throughout. Product composition as a separate object from outputs (which contains yields and quality) is a good design for structured data extraction.

Missing Properties: (1) No carrier gas flow rate — should be added to operating_conditions. (2) Product quality metrics (HHV, viscosity, density) are partially present but should be made explicit and required under outputs.product_quality. (3) Mass balance closure should be made required, not optional. (4) Catalyst characterization methods (BET, NH3-TPD, XRD, SEM) are tracked only as loading.surface_area — a full characterization_methods array is still absent.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Clear and practical with good examples. The catalyst activation_method and loading properties are important and well-defined. The agitation property under operating_conditions is unique and relevant for stirred batch reactors.
