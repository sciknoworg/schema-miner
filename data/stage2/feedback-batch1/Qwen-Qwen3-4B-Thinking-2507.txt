FEEDBACK:
The schema has improved considerably from Stage 1, now including a dedicated reactor object (configuration + scale) properly separated from process_parameters, catalyst characterization methods (BET, NH3-TPD, XRD, SEM, FT-IR), structured product composition with compound-concentration pairs for both liquid and gas fractions, product quality (HHV, viscosity), mass balance closure as a numeric field, pressure, and carrier gas flow rate.

Property Merging: No merging needed. The separation of reactor and process_parameters is now correct following Stage 1 feedback.

Property Grouping: The feedstock_preprocessing property exists at the top level rather than nested inside the feedstock object — it should be moved inside feedstock for logical consistency. All other groupings are appropriate.

Missing Properties: (1) Metadata is still minimal (experiment_id and date only) — author and DOI are essential for ORKG integration. (2) Feedstock contaminant tracking (chlorine ppm, heavy metals) is absent — critical for real mixed plastic waste with PVC. (3) No char composition properties (carbon content, ash content), which are consistently reported in the batch papers. (4) The catalyst_characterization enum is missing TGA, which is commonly used for coke quantification on spent catalysts.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Generally clear. The product quality requires HHV with a minimum of 37 MJ/kg — this is too high for mixed plastic waste oils containing PET or PVC fractions and should be lowered to 25 MJ/kg or removed. Temperature maximum should be raised from 600°C to 700°C.
