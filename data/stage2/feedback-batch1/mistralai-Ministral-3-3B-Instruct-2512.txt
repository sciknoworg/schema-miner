FEEDBACK:
The schema shows limited improvement from Stage 1. The overall structure remains similar with the same key issues persisting. Some positive additions are visible — the catalyst composition sub-object (metal_oxide, zeolite_type, active_species) is a creative and domain-relevant addition not seen in other schemas.

Property Merging: The catalyst "catalyst_form" property (fixed-bed, fluidized-bed, powder, pellet, monolithic) conflates catalyst physical morphology with reactor type — should be renamed "catalyst_morphology" and limited to physical forms only (powder, pellet, monolithic, granule). Heating_rate appears both in reactor.temperature.ramp_rate and process_parameters.heating_rate — this redundancy should be resolved by keeping it only in process_parameters.

Property Grouping: All thermal conditions (temperature, heating_rate, residence_time) should be consolidated in a single process_conditions object rather than split between reactor and process_parameters. This is the main structural issue.

Missing Properties: (1) Mass balance closure is still absent — this is a critical property for experimental validation. (2) No feedstock contaminant tracking (chlorine_ppm, heavy metals). (3) No carrier gas flow rate. (4) Product quality (HHV, viscosity, density) is absent — liquid products only capture compound name and yield percentage. (5) Catalyst characterization methods (BET, NH3-TPD, XRD, SEM) are still not tracked.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Clear. The temperature object with initial/final/ramp_rate is creative but non-standard — most papers report a single final temperature value. Consider simplifying to a single temperature field.
