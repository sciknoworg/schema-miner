FEEDBACK:
The schema has improved significantly from Stage 1, incorporating catalyst characterization (BET, NH3-TPD, XRD, SEM, FT-IR), metadata (study_id, author, DOI), feedstock preprocessing, product quality (HHV, viscosity, density), mass balance closure, and carrier gas flow rate. The structure is clean and well-organized across 11 top-level properties.

Property Merging: No merging needed. The separation of feedstock, catalyst, reactor, operational_conditions, product_yields, product_composition, and product_quality is logical and well-maintained.

Property Grouping: The grouping is appropriate. Product quality as a separate object from product yields and composition is a good design decision.

Missing Properties: (1) Feedstock contaminant tracking is absent — chlorine content (ppm) and heavy metals are critical for real mixed plastic waste containing PVC and multilayer packaging. (2) The analytical_characterisation enum is still too restrictive, missing GC-FID, GCxGC-FID/MS, BET, NH3-TPD, XRD, SEM, bomb calorimetry, and ASTM fuel tests (D2887, D4868, D4294). (3) The mass_balance_closure is a boolean (true/false) rather than a numeric value — a percentage number is far more informative and standard in the literature. (4) Feedstock preprocessing only allows a single method/temperature/duration object, but real papers often apply multiple sequential steps — should be an array.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Descriptions are thorough and clear with good use of units throughout. The temperature maximum of 600°C should be raised to 700°C to cover the full range reported in the literature.

ADDITIONAL COMMENTS:
The boolean mass_balance_closure is the most important fix needed — a numeric value (e.g., 97.5%) is standard in the literature and far more useful for data filtering and validation. The temperature ceiling of 600°C should also be raised to 700°C to avoid rejecting valid high-temperature experiments.
