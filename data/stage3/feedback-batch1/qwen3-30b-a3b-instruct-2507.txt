FEEDBACK:
The schema has incorporated two of the five items requested in Batch 2 feedback. Feedstock source is no longer required. The analyticalMethods.result field is no longer required — items now only require technique and sample, with result correctly optional. The atmosphere enum now includes "Inert". Char composition is correctly present under productComposition.

Property Merging: No merging needed. The camelCase naming convention is consistently maintained.

Property Grouping: Excellent. The separation of feedstock, catalyst, reactor, reactionConditions, productYields, productComposition, productQuality, analyticalMethods, and metadata remains the most structured design for literature-based data extraction.

Missing Properties — PREVIOUSLY REQUESTED AND STILL NOT INCORPORATED: (1) The reactionConditions.temperature range is still 200-800°C — correcting this to 300-700°C was requested in Batch 2 and has not been applied. (2) The feedstock.type and composition.polymer enums still do not include LDPE, HDPE, EPS, HIPS, or MLP — requested in Batch 2 and remains unresolved. (3) The metadata.doi field still uses "format": "uri" which rejects plain DOI strings — requested to be relaxed in Batch 2 and has not been changed.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Excellent. The char sub-object in productComposition and the gas quality metrics in productQuality are uniquely valuable and correctly maintained. The analyticalMethods.technique enum does not include NH3-TPD, which is consistently used for catalyst acidity characterization and is present in other schemas.
