FEEDBACK:
The schema has incorporated three of the five items requested in Batch 2 feedback. Catalyst characterization has been moved inside the catalyst object as a characterization array. Metadata now requires author and doi. The analytical_characterization.methods array now has a proper enum with standard techniques. Char composition (carbon_content_pct, ash_content_pct) is correctly present under products as a required field.

Property Merging: No merging needed.

Property Grouping: Grouping is appropriate. The catalyst.characterization placement is now correct.

Missing Properties — PREVIOUSLY REQUESTED AND STILL NOT INCORPORATED: (1) Density and chlorine_ppm of the liquid product were requested in Batch 2 — product_quality still only requires hhv and viscosity; density and chlorine_ppm are present but still not required. (2) The carrier_gas_type and carrier_gas_flow_rate remain as separate top-level fields rather than grouped inside a carrier_gas object nested in process_parameters or reactor — this structural issue was flagged in Batch 2 and remains unresolved.

Adequacy of Descriptions: Generally clear. The char_composition addition is a good improvement. The mass_balance_closure has maximum 100, which is too restrictive since experimental closure can legitimately exceed 100% due to measurement uncertainty — the maximum should be raised to at least 110.
